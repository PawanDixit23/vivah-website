To deliver deep matrimonial compatibility and maintain an authentic, bot-free community, we collect:

  • Identity & Verification Data: Legal full name, date of birth, gender, and government-issued ID proof (verified securely via encrypted DigiLocker / official APIs). Live video selfie liveness checks for photo authenticity.

Your data is processed strictly for authentic matrimonial matchmaking:

5. Core Privacy & Discretion Controls

Vivah provides industry-leading privacy innovations built directly into the app:

  • Ghost / Incognito Mode: Conceal your profile from discovery by phone contacts, office networks, or general browsing.
  • Photo Blur & Consent Shield: Your photos stay subtly blurred. Only profiles you accept can view your full gallery.
  • Anti-Screenshot Protection: Native screen capture prevention prevents other members from recording or taking screenshots of your photos or chats.
  • Parent & Family Viewer Mode: A safe viewing mode allowing parents to review astrological biodatas comfortably without displaying personal private chats.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks. Information is only shared with verified service providers (such as cloud hosting, payment gateways, and OTP service providers) under strict confidentiality agreements.

You have the absolute right to delete your profile at any time. When you choose to delete your Vivah account, your profile, photographs, Kundali records, and personal biodata are permanently purged from our active databases within 30 days, in accordance with applicable statutory guidelines.
