Child Protection & Safety Standards

Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Published in compliance with Google Play Child Safety Standards Policy, the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Vivah is exclusively an adult matrimonial and match-finding platform strictly intended for individuals seeking marriage who are 18 years of age or older. Vivah maintains a strict, unconditional zero-tolerance policy toward any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

Any user attempting to upload, share, solicit, promote, or link to content involving children or CSAE will face immediate and permanent account termination, device banning, and will be reported directly to law enforcement authorities including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local statutory bodies (such as cyber crime units in India).

2. Strict Age Verification & 18+ Age Gating

Under our Terms of Service and registration protocols:

  • Minors Prohibited: No individual under the age of 18 is permitted to register, create a profile, browse, or communicate on Vivah.
  • Government ID & Selfie Verification: Every matrimonial profile is subject to government photo ID verification (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License) which verifies legal birthdate and adult status.
  • Automated Gating: The sign-up process rejects any birthdate indicating an age under 18 years old.

3. Proactive Content Moderation & Image Screening

To ensure our platform remains safe, verified, and free of harmful content:

  • Media Screening: All photos uploaded to profiles are screened before or immediately upon publication to ensure no abusive, exploitative, or minor imagery exists.
  • Automated & Human Review: Moderation systems and human trust-and-safety specialists monitor reports and flag suspicious accounts promptly.
  • Immediate Takedown: Any content flagged as potential CSAE/CSAM is instantly quarantined, permanently deleted from public view, and preserved for law enforcement handoff.

4. In-App Reporting Mechanism

Vivah provides accessible, simple tools for users to report safety violations:

  • In-App Reporting: Every user profile and chat window contains a prominent "Report Profile" button where safety concerns, impersonation, or suspicious content can be reported 24/7 with a single tap.
  • Expedited Priority Review: Any report categorized under child safety or abuse is treated with the highest severity tier and investigated within minutes by our Trust & Safety team.

5. Cooperation with Law Enforcement & Statutory Authorities

Vivah fully complies with all applicable local, national, and international child protection laws, including:

  • Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 & Intermediary Guidelines (Rule 3(1)(b))
  • Mandatory reporting and full cooperation with law enforcement agencies and cyber-crime departments upon receipt of lawful notices.
